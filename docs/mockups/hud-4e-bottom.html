D&D 4e HUD across the bottom: three layouts (mockup)

Placed as in the owner's screenshot: the HUD fills the bottom band from the left edge to past the hotbar (yellow), Foundry's players list moves up into the free corner above it (red, dashed outline), the hotbar lives inside the HUD. Each layout is shown dark (Aegustarion, the elf ranger) and light (Linkedin, the elf bard), with powers from their sheets; HP, defences and other numbers are made up. Power names drop the compendium prefix ("Ranger 01-Twin Strike" → Twin Strike).
