D&D 4e HUD for monsters (mockup)

The PC HUD as released (v0.43.6, its own stylesheet), changed for NPCs as agreed (Q155–Q158): the name plate in the role's colour (gold edge for elites and solos) with a line "Level 3 Elite Brute (Leader) · 300 XP"; HP with bloodied, minions say "Minion"; conditions plus resist / vulnerable / immune on one line; saving throws and AP with Spend; speed, initiative, Perception; the defences. Tabs Standard, Move, Minor, Triggered, Traits; columns At-Will, Recharge (with encounter powers), Basic. A used recharge power shows its dice and a Roll button. GM only. The monster is made up.
