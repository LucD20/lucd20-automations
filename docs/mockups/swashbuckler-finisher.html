Swashbuckler Finisher

A study for a signature animation: it plays once, when the damage of a Strike made with a finisher is rolled. The dice flourish, a cut-in strip names the finisher, the blade carves a mark on the target, the screen freezes into a black-and-white impact frame, and then the target's token art is thrown like a coin, as in Token Reactions. Each part can be switched off below, so you can pick the mix you like.

Drag the swashbuckler or a target to another square. Where they stand decides which way the target is thrown.
