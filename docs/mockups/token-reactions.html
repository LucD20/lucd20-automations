Token Reactions

Mockup for LucD20 Automations: the hit token reacts like a card.

Drag either token. The hit comes from Valeros and knocks the target away from him.

What moves: only the drawing. The token's art is lifted like a card, tilted in perspective (drawn on a bendable mesh), knocked back and shaken, then it settles. The token itself never moves.

In Foundry it would play on critical hits, kills and hits of a quarter of max HP or more, for everyone who can see the target. With JB2A installed, a matching JB2A impact clip would play on top of this. JB2A's files can't be loaded in this page.
